# BendBuddy: realistic $0-first monetization plan

## Current position
BendBuddy is a free static web app hosted on GitHub Pages. Current tools include offset and 90-degree stub-up calculators, bend diagrams, quick guides, and searchable electrician field sheets. The app has a Roadmap tab explaining that monetization is not active yet and a Share button to help test demand.

## The realistic strategy
**Do not add a paywall yet.** First prove that electricians use the app repeatedly. A working tool that saves a little time can earn trust; charging before users trust it makes adoption harder.

### Phase 1 — Validate (weeks 1–2, $0)
- Share https://967wsfvrcf-glitch.github.io/BendBuddy/ with 10–20 electricians/apprentices.
- Ask three questions: Which tool did you use? What was confusing or missing? Would a specific advanced feature be worth paying for?
- Keep a simple note in iPhone Notes with feedback and how often people use the app.
- Don't collect sensitive jobsite information or claim code compliance.

### Phase 2 — Improve the most-requested feature (weeks 2–4, $0)
Prioritize one repeat-use feature, such as more bend types, better step-by-step diagrams, saved favorites/settings, or printable reference sheets. Test it with a few people before expanding.

### Phase 3 — Try a small paid add-on only if users ask (after validation)
Possible low-complexity experiments:
- A one-time downloadable pack of printable bend worksheets and field checklists.
- An optional paid collection of advanced calculators/visual guides while core references remain free.
- A voluntary tip/support link.

Payment providers generally take fees from transactions and may have eligibility, tax, and payout requirements. Compare terms before choosing one; don't promise earnings and don't pay for subscriptions or ads upfront. Do not add a payment link until an account is configured and you have verified the checkout works.

## Possible income expectations
Revenue is uncertain and could be $0. Example math only: 10 sales of a $5 digital pack = $50 gross before platform/payment fees and taxes; 50 sales = $250 gross before fees and taxes. These are scenarios, not forecasts. Getting consistent users is likely harder than writing the code.

## Keep costs and risks low
- Keep the static site on GitHub Pages while it meets your needs.
- Don't buy ads, a domain, a paid app-builder plan, or subscriptions before validating demand.
- If you add affiliate links or sponsorships later, disclose the relationship clearly.
- Never monetize by presenting unverified electrical guidance as code-approved advice. Keep warnings and ask qualified electricians to review technical content.
- If a paid service, email list, or backend is introduced, explain privacy practices and any ongoing cost first.

## First action
Use the app's Roadmap tab → Share BendBuddy. Send the link to a handful of electricians and ask what would save them time. Build the most-requested feature before taking payment.
