Quick field guide

Common EMT rules of thumb
  • Offset: spacing = rise × multiplier.
  • 10°: multiplier 5.76; approximate shrink 1/16 in per inch of rise.
  • 22.5°: multiplier 2.6; approximate shrink 3/16 in per inch of rise.
  • 30°: multiplier 2; approximate shrink 1/4 in per inch of rise.
  • 45°: multiplier 1.4; approximate shrink 3/8 in per inch of rise.
  • 60°: multiplier 1.155; approximate shrink 1/2 in per inch of rise.
  • 90° stub: mark = desired stub height − bender take-up.

These are approximate field formulas. Follow the bender's markings, plans, applicable code, and employer procedures. Verify before cutting or bending.
